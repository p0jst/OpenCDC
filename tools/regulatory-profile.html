2 · Regulatory hooks per framework function

GOVERN

  • Management bodies must approve measures, oversee implementation and follow training; personal liability possible, per NIS2 Art. 20. NIS2
  • All-hazards, proportionate risk-management approach, per NIS2 Art. 21(1). NIS2
  • Management body bears final responsibility for ICT risk, per DORA Art. 5. DORA
  • Controller accountability and appropriate measures, per GDPR Art. 24 & 32. GDPR

IDENTIFY

  • Risk analysis and vulnerability handling policies, per NIS2 Art. 21(2)(a),(e); supplier and dependency knowledge, per Art. 21(2)(d). NIS2
  • Records of processing double as data-flow inventory, per GDPR Art. 30. GDPR
  • Identification of ICT-supported functions and information assets, per DORA Art. 8. DORA
  • Security requirements for products with digital elements feed procurement criteria. CRA

PROTECT

  • Cryptography policies, access control, MFA, hygiene & training, per NIS2 Art. 21(2)(f)–(j). NIS2
  • Encryption and pseudonymisation as named measures, per GDPR Art. 32. GDPR
  • Protection and prevention measures, per DORA Art. 9. DORA
  • Physical resilience measures for critical entities complement cyber controls. CER

DETECT

  • Reporting deadlines are only achievable with functioning detection, per NIS2 Art. 21(2)(b), 23. NIS2
  • Detection speed determines 72 h feasibility; security logs about employees are personal data, so purpose-limit them. Per GDPR Art. 33 and Art. 5. GDPR
  • Prompt anomaly-detection mechanisms, per DORA Art. 10. DORA

RESPOND

  • Incident handling and staged reporting, per NIS2 Art. 21(2)(b), 23. NIS2
  • Notify DPA ≤ 72 h; document all breaches, even non-notified, per GDPR Art. 33(5), 34. GDPR
  • Incident management, classification and reporting, per DORA Art. 17–19. DORA

RECOVER

  • Business continuity, backups, disaster recovery and crisis management, per NIS2 Art. 21(2)(c); final report ≤ 1 month, per Art. 23. NIS2
  • Ability to restore availability and access to personal data in a timely manner, per GDPR Art. 32(1)(c). GDPR
  • Response & recovery plans, backup policies and testing, per DORA Art. 11–12. DORA
  • Continuity of essential societal functions under all-hazards scenarios. CER
