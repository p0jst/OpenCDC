Maturity Self-Assessment

Pick a function, then click a status for each criterion; the score and open actions update as you go. A level counts only when every criterion at that level and below is implemented: staged scoring, no averaging up. See the maturity model. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Assessment details and how scoring works · assessors, date, evidence location

Each criterion is scored on a five-point scale. Only “implemented” and “implemented & evidenced” count towards a level. The three states below them record work in flight without moving the score, because the maturity model is staged and does not average up. See the scoring method.
